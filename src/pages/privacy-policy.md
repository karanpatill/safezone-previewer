This policy explains how SafeZoneSnap (“we”, “us”, safezonesnap.com) handles information when you use the website. The short version: **the files you preview never leave your device**, we do not run accounts, and the only third-party data processing on the site is for advertising.

## 1. Files you load into the previewer

Images and videos you open, drop or paste into the previewer are read by your browser using standard web APIs (File, Blob URLs, HTML5 video and Canvas) and rendered on your own device. They are **not uploaded, transmitted, stored or analysed by us or anyone else**. When you close or reload the page, the browser discards them. Images you download from the previewer are generated locally in the same way.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The previewer saves your display preferences (selected platform, enabled layers, interface opacity, scaling mode and custom margins) in your browser’s `localStorage` under the key `safezonesnap:v1`, so the tool looks the same next time you visit. This data never leaves your device and contains no personal information. You can delete it at any time by clearing site data in your browser or by clicking **Reset** in the tool.

## 3. Information collected automatically

Like every website, our hosting provider receives standard technical information when your browser requests a page: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring page and the time of the request. This is used to deliver the site, protect it against abuse and diagnose errors, and it is kept in server logs for a limited period. We do not use it to identify you.

## 4. Advertising and cookies

We use **Google AdSense** to show advertising, which pays for the site. Google and its partners act as third-party vendors and use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, and similar technologies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

- Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google’s [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) or, in Europe, at [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/).
- For more on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message from a Google-certified consent management platform before personalised advertising cookies are set. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use cookies only for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention.

## 5. Legal bases (GDPR and UK GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on **legitimate interests** to operate, secure and improve the website (server logs), on **consent** for personalised advertising cookies, and on **your request** when you email us. You can withdraw consent at any time through the consent message or your browser settings.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port personal data we hold about you, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we hold almost no personal data (we have no accounts and never receive your files), most requests will concern emails you have sent us.

**California residents (CCPA/CPRA):** you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, to correct inaccurate information, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising cookies set by Google may count as “sharing” under California law. You can opt out using the links in section 4, or by enabling the Global Privacy Control signal in your browser, which we treat as a valid opt-out request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

**California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA):** you can visit the site anonymously. This policy is linked from the footer of every page, and we will notify you of changes by updating this page and its “Last updated” date. We honour Global Privacy Control as described above; the older Do Not Track browser setting is not consistently supported by advertising vendors.

To make a request, email [support@safezonesnap.com](mailto:support@safezonesnap.com) with the subject “Privacy request”. We respond within 30 days, or within the period your local law requires.

## 7. Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Emails you send us

If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to improve the tool, keep it no longer than that purpose needs, and never add you to a mailing list.

## 9. International transfers

Our hosting and advertising providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the “Last updated” date above.
