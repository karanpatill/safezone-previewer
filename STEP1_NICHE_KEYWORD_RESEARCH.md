## 1. Problem Identification
- **Pain Point:** Over 120M content creators on Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok constantly struggle with UI overlays. When a video is uploaded, the app overlays:
  - Top header (search, camera, following/for you tabs)
  - Right sidebar (profile avatar, like button, comment bubble, share arrow, bookmark, audio disc)
  - Bottom zone (creator username, 2-line caption, music title scrolling ticker)
- Creators frequently find their text, subtitles, call-to-actions, and key visual elements blocked or cropped.
- **Current Competitors' Flaw:** Existing sites (e.g. random design blogs) only offer a downloadable `.png` template. Creators have to download it, open Premiere/CapCut/Canva, import it as an extra track, and align it.
- **Our Disruptive Solution:** A 100% free, instant, client-side web tool where creators can drag-and-drop their image or video, toggle between Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts UI overlays live, toggle rule-of-thirds grid, and instantly verify safe margins in seconds.

## 3. Safe Zone Technical Specifications (2026 Updated)
- **Base Resolution:** 1080 × 1920 px (9:16 vertical aspect ratio).
- **Instagram Reels Safe Area:**
  - Top Margin: 220 px (Avoid top account and search bar)
  - Bottom Margin: 420 px (Avoid caption, audio title, and progress bar)
  - Right Margin: 140 px (Avoid avatar, like, comment, and share buttons)
  - Left Margin: 60 px (Clear safe padding)
  - Feed Crop Zone: 1080 × 1350 px (4:5 grid preview) and 1080 × 1080 px (1:1 profile grid)
- **TikTok Safe Area:**
  - Top Margin: 180 px
